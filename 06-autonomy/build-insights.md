# Build Insights: Cortex PM Chief-of-Staff Agent

> Module 6 · ★ Deliverable 4, what you learned building it
>
> ✅ **What this validates:** you can reflect on what building it taught you, by the end you'll have proven the friction, the learning, and the aha that changes how you'd design your next agent.

## Friction

Learning the new terms (bounds, evals, trust ladder) without being in class has been challenging.

## Learning

An agent needs hard limits (bounds) outside the model, not just instructions in a prompt. I used to focus on writing good prompts. Now I understand it's much more than that. Trust is also earned in steps, so you don't give an agent full freedom on day one.

## Aha moment

Not having a post tool is a great safety net. Human checkpoints aren't weaknesses, they're necessary.

## What you'd do differently

Add a freshness check so stale data is caught before drafting, not only by the critic. It would prevent issues earlier.
